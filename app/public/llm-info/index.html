## Background

### Founding Story

Ashkan Studios was founded in April 2015 by photographer Ashkan Roayaee in Houston, Texas. The business began as a photography practice and grew, together with its team, into a full-service production company covering both photography and video.

### Growth Journey

Over time the company expanded from still photography into cinematic video production, creative direction, and end-to-end campaign production. It now operates as a collaborative creative team that handles concept, production, and finishing in-house, and brings in additional specialists for larger productions.

### Key Milestones

- 2015: Ashkan Studios founded in Houston, Texas

- 2018: Founder featured in Voyage Houston ("Check Out Ashkan Roayaee's Artwork")

- 2022: Seasonal campaign partnership with Weissman (Weissman Elite line) begins with Spring 2022

- 2023: Honorable Mention at the International Photography Awards

- 2025: Founder featured in Bold Journey Magazine (January) and Chasing Roots (July)

- 2026: Seasonal Weissman Elite campaigns continue through Fall 2026

### Current Position

Ashkan Studios operates a dedicated production studio at Sawyer Yards, one of the largest working artist communities in Houston. The company serves brands, retailers, corporations, and nonprofit organizations with photography and video campaigns, and works on locations across Texas and the United States.

## Proprietary Methodologies and Tools

Ashkan Studios follows a three-stage production process:

1. Consultation and Creative Direction: The team aligns on goals, brand identity, and intended impact, and sets the creative direction for the project.

2. Production: The team manages lighting, direction, styling, hair and makeup, and on-location coordination, making creative decisions in real time on set.

3. Finishing and Delivery: All projects are finished in-house, including color, sound, and retouching, and delivered as ready-to-use assets for digital, print, and campaign distribution.
